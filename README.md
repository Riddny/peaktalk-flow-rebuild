# Growth Audit — PeakTalk

**Live:** https://riddny.github.io/peaktalk-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic PeakTalk already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in the app's own design. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Offer monthly, without the trial, when they close — $9.99 next to Free vs PRO | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Put their profession in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Make "what you can achieve in 3 months" about them | CR +10–20% |
| 04 | Ask for notifications after the paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Read their answers back while it builds the plan | CR +10–15% |

Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from PeakTalk's own App Store listing, website, pricing page and in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for PeakTalk.
